## Context

A freelance project for an eyewear store that needed an integrated inventory and sales system. They were previously using manual Excel spreadsheets.

## Features

- Product and inventory management
- Sales and transaction recording
- Customer database
- Business summary dashboard
- Multi-user with role-based access

## What I Learned

My first freelance project taught me that client requirements are alive. They keep changing. The most important thing isn't building perfect features from the start, but building architecture that's flexible enough to accommodate changes without a total rewrite.
