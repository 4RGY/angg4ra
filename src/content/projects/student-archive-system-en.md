## Concept

Managing student data manually tends to scatter and lose structure. This project is an archiving system for storing and managing student records in one tidy, searchable place.

## Approach

- **Full CRUD** — create, read, update, and delete archive records.
- **Structured storage** — data kept tidy and searchable.
- **Simple interface** — focused on a clear archiving workflow.

## Reflection

Internal systems like this teach that an app's value often lives in data orderliness, not flashy features.
