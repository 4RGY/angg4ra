## The Story

My friend (Piti) kept getting confused about her class schedule. Instead of answering the same questions repeatedly, I just built a small app she could check anytime.

## Features

- Clean daily schedule view
- Works on both mobile and desktop
- Schedule data is easy to update
- No backend needed, pure frontend

## Why It's Here

Small project, but it proves that solutions don't need to be complex. Sometimes all you need is one HTML page that solves a real person's problem.
