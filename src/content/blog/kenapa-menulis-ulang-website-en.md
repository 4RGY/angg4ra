My previous portfolio website had a strong identity, but it felt heavy. Not heavy in the slow-loading sense, but visually heavy: too many colors, too much decoration, too many things competing for attention.

## Seeing the problem

I sat down and looked at it honestly. There was a grain overlay, three ambient orbs, several accent colors used together. Every element had a reason to exist, but together they drowned each other out.

The content (projects, writing, identity) was buried under layers of decoration.

## The decision to simplify

This redesign started from one principle: *every element must have a job.* If something doesn't help communicate the content, it goes.

A controlled palette. Generous whitespace. Clear typography. Sharp, purposeful motion instead of animation for its own sake.

## Speed as a feature

Astro with islands means pages load almost instantly. JavaScript loads only when needed. This isn't mere technical optimization; it's part of the experience. A fast website respects the visitor's time.

## What I learned

Rewriting something that already "works" is scary, but often necessary. The best version of a piece isn't the busiest; it's the one that communicates its intent most clearly.
